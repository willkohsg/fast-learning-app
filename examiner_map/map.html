Additional Mathematics 4049 · Sec 3 End-of-Year · Construction grammar

Drawn from Fuhua Secondary's 2024 (3NA) and 2025 (3E) End-of-Year papers. This is the construction grammar — the habits that repeat regardless of which topic is being tested. Learn the grammar and the paper stops being a surprise.

1  Structural signature

ElementFuhua's habit
OpenerA 2–4 mark algebra warm-up. Quadratic inequality (2025 Q1a), 4x² < x (2024 Q3), principal value (2024 Q1a). Deliberately low-stakes.
DiscriminantAppears twice, in two disguises: once as a proof ("show … for all real k"), once as a range or count of intersections. 2025 Q1b + Q2; 2024 Q6 + Q8.
SpineOne 10–12 mark coordinate-geometry question, headed "Solutions by accurate drawing will not be accepted".
CloserThe trigonometric graph — amplitude, period, sketch. Nearly identical two years running.
Ladder1–2 mark openers → 4–6 mark middle → one 10–12 mark spine.

2  The five recurring construction devices

  1. 1  A prose-reasoning mark in every paper (~2 marks). Not computation — explanation.
    • 2025 Q5(i): "David claims … Explain why his claim is wrong."
    • 2024 Q2(ii): "Explain why y = −7 is the only real root."
    • 2024 Q15(b): "Explain why y = 1 is not a tangent."
  2. 2  A "for all values of" proof. They want the discriminant shown positive or negative, with justification — not merely non-zero. 2025 Q1(b).
  3. 3  A context-ceiling answer. "the number of complete hours" (2025 Q4d). The decimal is not the answer; the next whole unit is.
  4. 4  Deliberately ugly exact values. B(28/9, 4/3) in 2025 Q9(iii). The method is standard; the arithmetic is the filter.
  5. 5  Answer-form constraints, always stated. "in the form a + b√3 where a and b are integers" / "rational numbers" / "correct to 2 decimal places" / "in radians as a multiple of π" / "exact form". A correct number in the wrong form loses the A mark.

3  Topic frame (Express)

Quadratics & discriminant · surds (always both rationalising and a surd equation) · polynomials (remainder / factor → solve cubic) · partial fractions (always with an irreducible quadratic factor) · indices & logarithms (including change of base) · exponential growth model · binomial theorem · coordinate geometry · circles · linear law · trigonometric ratios with negative / complementary angles · trigonometric graphs.
